## Overview

Use this template to register a **Business Domain** in the Witboost catalog.

### What's a Template?

A Template is a tool that helps create components inside a Data Landscape. Templates help establish a standard across the organisation. This standard leads to easier understanding, management and maintenance of components. Templates provide a predefined structure so that developers don't have to start from scratch each time, which leads to faster development and allows them to focus on other aspects, such as testing and business logic.

For more information, please refer to the [official documentation](https://docs.witboost.com/docs/data-teams/p6_advanced/p6_1_templates#getting-started).

### What's a Business Domain?

A Business Domain is a **catalog entity** that represents an organisational or functional area of your company (e.g. Finance, HR, Supply Chain). Business Domains act as containers for Data Products and can be nested: a domain can be declared as a sub-domain of a broader domain (called a Business Field).

Domains must exist before Data Products can be created inside them.
